UT Fall 2026 calendar
=====================

File to import: Fall-2026-Outlook-Calendar.ics
Time zone: Central Time (America/Chicago)

The colored square in every title is the class. The word after it is the activity
(Lecture, Exam, Quiz, HW due, Lab, Studio, Office hours, Reading due, Project due).
That is readable the moment you import it.

Suggested Outlook category colors, set once:
  ARE 102    blue
  ARE 217    purple
  CE 311K    orange
  CE 311S    green
  EM 306     red
  ME 310T    yellow
  UT Austin  dark gray

Outlook shows one color bar per event, so the bar is the class. Search the calendar
for "Exam" or "HW due" when you only want that kind of item.

How to put it in Outlook
------------------------

Outlook on the web:
  1. Open calendar.outlook.com.
  2. Add calendar, then Create blank calendar. Name it Fall 2026 Classes.
  3. Add calendar, then Upload from file. Choose Fall-2026-Outlook-Calendar.ics
     and put it on the Fall 2026 Classes calendar.

Outlook desktop:
  1. File, Open & Export, Import/Export.
  2. Import an iCalendar (.ics) or vCalendar file.
  3. Choose Fall-2026-Outlook-Calendar.ics.
  4. If it asks, import into a new calendar named Fall 2026 Classes.

On Sunday, August 23, open the all-day event "Start here". It repeats these steps.
